End-user questions answered

How does this help me?

It reduces food waste, saves money, and removes the daily stress of deciding what to cook.

How does the app know what is available?

Users can scan fridge photos, upload grocery receipts, connect shopping lists, or add items manually. The app asks for confirmation when it is unsure.

Will this make life easier or more difficult?

Easier. The goal is fewer decisions: Scrappy suggests meals automatically and highlights only the ingredients that need attention.

What if I do not like an ingredient?

You can mark dislikes, allergies, dietary preferences, and cooking skill level. Scrappy adapts suggestions.

What makes it different from a normal recipe app?

Normal apps start with “What do you want to eat?” Scrappy starts with “What do you already have before it expires?”

Publishing plan

How Scrappy becomes an iOS and Android app

This section directly answers the app store question in the feedback.

1. Build mobile app

Use React Native with Expo for one codebase across iOS and Android.

2. Add camera tools

Use Expo Camera or Vision Camera for scanning food, labels, barcodes, and receipts.

3. Connect AI vision

Use Gemini Vision or OpenAI Vision to recognise items and expiry information.

4. Store user pantry

Use Firebase or Supabase so pantry data syncs across devices.

5. Publish

Build releases with Expo EAS, then submit to App Store Connect and Google Play Console.
